Automatic Google Sheet updates

Your published Google Sheet is checked automatically when you open this app and when your connection returns. You can still press the button below to refresh manually. Saved learning progress, notes, and existing deck positions are retained.

Your sheet is published to the web, so anyone with its published link may be able to read the vocabulary. This connection is read-only: changes made in the app do not write back to the sheet.

Or import a CSV file

Choose a CSV file exported from Excel or Google Sheets. The first row should contain column names.

✨ Add a word prepared by ChatGPT

Ask ChatGPT to research a new word, review the details, and approve it. Then copy the tab-separated row ChatGPT gives you and paste it below. The app will add it to your local vocabulary.

Field order: German → English → Category → Article/full form → Example → Translation → Grammar explanation → Your notes → Pronunciation → Importance → For exam (Yes/No) → Azeez suggestion (Yes/No). You can paste multiple rows, one word per line.

Your data & privacy

Your words, settings and study history are stored in your browser. When you use an account, vocabulary progress may also be synchronized with the connected cloud service (Supabase). Shared vocabulary updates load from a published Google Sheet. This is a learning app, not an official telc or BAMF exam product.

You can export a copy of your vocabulary and learning progress. Deleting browser data can remove information stored only on this device. Before changing devices, export a backup and verify cloud synchronization if you use it.

Reset learning progress

Only the selected progress is reset. Vocabulary, bookmarks and fixed deck assignments are preserved. Learned status is shared between vocabulary, flashcards and quizzes.
